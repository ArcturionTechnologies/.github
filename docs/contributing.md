# 🤝 Join the conversation

Arcturion Technologies is a personal development project. Thoughtful, respectful collaboration is welcome.

## Helpful contributions

- Explain a confusing step in a workflow using a fictional example.
- Suggest clearer wording, simpler navigation, or a more manageable task sequence.
- Point out a broken link, incorrect statement, or missing evidence.
- Share a design question or a reproducible issue in material that is already public.

[Open a documentation or design issue](https://github.com/ArcturionTechnologies/.github/issues/new)

## Keep it safe and useful

Describe the problem in general terms. Do not attach private documents, account details, medical or legal records, credentials, identifying logs, or private venture material. A fictional example is usually enough to discuss the design.

No diagnosis or personal history is required to give accessibility feedback.

## The atmosphere we want

Treat people with dignity. Be curious about different ways of thinking and working. Critique the idea or behavior with specific evidence. Make room for disagreement, correction, and peaceful problem-solving.

Harassment, threats, disclosure of another person's private information, and coercive behavior do not belong in this project space.

The project is maintained as a personal effort; response times and implementation of suggestions are not guaranteed.
