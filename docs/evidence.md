## What remains unestablished

The showcase does not establish broad accuracy, universal approval enforcement, accessibility outcomes, time savings, benefits for a particular population, user adoption, or commercial readiness.

A useful next step for the mission would be testing a defined task with the people it is intended to support, recording both successful outcomes and the human effort or intervention required.
