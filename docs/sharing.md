# 🔒 Sharing with care

Public learning should not require exposing someone's life or unpublished work.

## Suitable material for this showcase

- Fictional or synthetic examples.
- Abstract diagrams and descriptions of design decisions.
- Scoped verification summaries with dates and limitations.
- Public links whose content is appropriate for the showcase.
- Original explanatory notes prepared for public review.

## Material that stays private

Personal records, credentials, private ventures, proprietary implementation, internal control policies, operational logs, and identifying configuration are excluded.

Publishing an abstract pattern does not require publishing the private system that uses it. A public note should explain the problem, the approach, and the evidence at an appropriate level.

## Before a contribution is public

Check the content and its history for sensitive information. Verify the origin and sharing rights of any reused material. Replace real examples with synthetic ones, and make the status of the example explicit. Review dates, claims, links, and the intended audience.

Keep private records out of public issues, comments, screenshots, and pull requests.
